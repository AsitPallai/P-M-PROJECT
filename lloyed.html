Diesel Engine Interview Questions and Answers

🔧 Basic Diesel Engine Interview Questions

1. What is the basic difference between a diesel engine and a petrol engine?
Diesel engines use compression ignition and inject fuel directly into the cylinder, whereas petrol engines use spark ignition and mix air-fuel before combustion.
2. How does a diesel engine work?
It works on the compression ignition principle: air is compressed, heating it up, and diesel is injected into this hot air, causing spontaneous combustion.
3. What are the four strokes in a four-stroke diesel engine?
Intake, Compression, Power, and Exhaust.
4. Why don't diesel engines use spark plugs?
Diesel ignites through heat from compression, not a spark. Spark plugs are not needed.
5. What is the purpose of a fuel injector in a diesel engine?
To atomize and deliver fuel directly into the combustion chamber at high pressure.
6. What is meant by compression ignition?
It is ignition caused by the high temperature of compressed air, not a spark.
7. Why do diesel engines have higher compression ratios than petrol engines?
Higher compression improves thermal efficiency and enables compression ignition.
8. What are the main components of a diesel engine?
Cylinder block, piston, crankshaft, fuel injector, camshaft, valves, turbocharger (if fitted), and fuel pump.
9. Why do diesel engines produce black smoke?
Black smoke is caused by incomplete combustion due to excess fuel or insufficient air.
10. How is power output controlled in a diesel engine?
By regulating the quantity of fuel injected into the cylinder.

🧰 Technical and Diagnostic Questions

1. What are common reasons for a diesel engine not starting?
Air in fuel line, low compression, weak battery, bad fuel pump, faulty glow plugs, or clogged filters.
2. What causes a diesel engine to misfire?
Injector issues, air/fuel imbalance, faulty fuel pump, or low compression.
3. What are the symptoms of a bad fuel injector?
Rough idle, misfire, poor fuel economy, black smoke, or engine knock.
4. What is turbocharging in diesel engines and how does it work?
A turbo uses exhaust gases to spin a turbine, compressing intake air and improving engine power and efficiency.
5. What are the signs of a failing turbocharger?
Whining noise, loss of power, black smoke, oil in intercooler, or high exhaust temperature.
6. How do you perform a compression test on a diesel engine?
Remove injectors, attach a diesel compression tester, crank the engine, and record pressure values per cylinder.
7. What could cause a diesel engine to lose power under load?
Clogged filters, turbo failure, injector issues, air leaks, or EGR problems.
8. What is the function of the EGR system?
Recirculates a portion of exhaust gases back into the intake to reduce NOx emissions.
9. What is diesel knock and what causes it?
A loud knocking sound due to delayed combustion, often caused by poor fuel quality or injector timing issues.
10. What is the function of a common rail in a diesel injection system?
It stores high-pressure fuel and supplies it to injectors, allowing precise control over injection timing and quantity.

🧠 Conceptual and Application Questions

1. Explain the working of a common rail direct injection (CRDI) system.
CRDI uses a common high-pressure fuel rail to supply fuel to solenoid-controlled injectors, allowing multi-stage precise injection.
2. What is BSFC (Brake Specific Fuel Consumption)?
BSFC is the amount of fuel consumed per unit of power produced, measured in g/kWh. It indicates engine efficiency.
3. How does intercooling improve diesel engine performance?
It cools the compressed intake air from the turbo, increasing air density and combustion efficiency.
4. What are the advantages and disadvantages of diesel engines over petrol engines?
Advantages: More torque, better fuel efficiency, longer engine life.
Disadvantages: Heavier, more expensive, higher emissions of NOx and particulates.
5. Why are diesel engines preferred for heavy-duty applications?
Because they produce higher torque at low RPM, are more fuel-efficient, and have longer durability.

🛠️ Practical/Hands-On Questions

1. How would you troubleshoot a diesel engine that emits white smoke?
Check for unburnt fuel (cold cylinder), faulty injectors, low compression, or coolant leakage into the cylinder.
2. Describe the steps to bleed air from a diesel fuel system.
  • Fill fuel tank
  • Open bleed screws
  • Use primer pump until fuel flows bubble-free
  • Tighten screws and repeat if necessary
3. How do you check injector spray pattern?
Remove the injector and test it using an injector tester or test bench to observe spray and pressure.
4. What safety precautions should be taken while working on diesel engines?
  • Disconnect battery before work
  • Wear gloves and eye protection
  • Ensure proper ventilation
  • Never open pressurized lines when hot
5. How do you maintain a diesel engine for long life and efficiency?
  • Regular oil and filter changes
  • Clean air filters
  • Timely fuel filter replacement
  • Monitor coolant and turbo systems
  • Periodic injector and valve inspections

The swing motor enables 360° rotation of the upper structure. A slow swing could be caused by:

  • Contaminated hydraulic oil
  • Faulty swing control valve
  • Worn swing motor or gear
Troubleshooting involves pressure tests, oil analysis, and valve/motor inspection.
