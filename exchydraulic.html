Main componates of Hydraulic System in ZX220 LC

  1. Hydraulic pump
    • Type: Variable Displacement axial Piston Pump(Usually 2 pumps for working + 1 pilot pump).
    • Function : Converts engines mechanical power into hydraulic oil pressure

  2. Pilot Pump
    • Supplies low pressure oil (3-4 Mpa) to the pilot circuit (control levers, joysticks).
    • Helps in smooth operation of main control valves.

  3. Main control valve
    • A multi section valve controlling flow to each actuator (boom, arm,bucket, swing, travel motors).
    • operates proportionally with joysticks input.

  4. Hydraulic Cylinders
    • Boom Cylinder- Lifting / lowering
    • Arm cylinders- digging / reaching.
    • Bucket Cylinder- Scopping / dumping

  5. Swing motor & travel motors
    • Swing Motor:Rotates upper structure.
    • Travel Motors: Propel tracks forword / backward.

  6. Hydraulic oil tank & filters
    • Stores oil and removes impurities.
    • Equipped with suction and return filters for protection

  7. Relief valves
    • Protects system from over pressure (safety)
    • Set pressure usually around 34-35 Mpa (working circuit)<./li>

  8. Hydrauling Cooling system
    • Oil cooler + radiator to maintain optimum oil temperature.

Working principle

  1. The engine drives the hydraulic pumps.
  2. Pumps delivers pressurized oil - main control valve.
  3. Oil onputs via joysticks (pilot circuit ) - directs oil to required cylinder/motor.
  4. Oil pressure moves cylinder or motors (boom, arm, bucket, swing, travel).
  5. Return oil goes back to tank throgh return filter.
  6. Relief valves & sensors maintain safe pressure.

Typical Hydraulic specifications (ZX220LC)

  • Main pump flow : 2 x 220 L/min (variable depending on load).
  • Pilot pump pressure : 3.5 Mpa.
  • Main relief valve pressure: 34.3 Mpa.
  • Swing motor pressure : 27-30 Mpa.

Interview question and answer:

  1. What type of hydraulic pump is used in Zx220LC.

    • Answer:

      It uses two variable displcement axial piston pumps for the main circuit and one gear-type pilot pump.The main pumps supply oil to actuators, while the pilot pump powers control levers and proportaional valves.

  2. How is the flow shared between boom, arm, bucket, and travel functions?

    • Answer:

      The Electronoc pump control (EPC) and main control valve distribute flow according to operator joysticks input.

  3. What is the function of the pilot hydraulic system?
    • Answer:

      The pilot system provides low-pressure oil to joysticks and pedals, which then control the main control valve spools. This allows smooth, precise actuation of cylinders and motors.


  4. What are common hydraulic problems in ZX220 LC?
    • Answer:
      • Slow operation of boom/arm → pump wear, clogged filters, or low oil level.
      • Machine jerking → air in hydraulic lines, faulty relief valve.
      • Excessive heating → cooler blockage, overloading, low oil.
      • Weak travel power → pump wear, leakage in travel motor.

  5. How do you check if a hydraulic pump is weak?
    • Answer:
      • Measure pump delivery pressure & flow under load.
      • If pressure does not reach set value (34 MPa) or flow drops, the pump is worn.
      • Also check for abnormal noise, overheating, or cavitation.

  6. What is the function of a relief valve in this system?
    • Answer:
      • Protects system from overpressure.
      • When set pressure (34 MPa) is exceeded, the valve opens and diverts excess oil to the tank.

  7. Why does hydraulic oil overheat in excavators?
    • Answer:
      • Overloading or continuous high-pressure operation.
      • Blocked hydraulic cooler.
      • Low oil level or wrong oil grade.
      • Internal leakage in cylinders or motors.
